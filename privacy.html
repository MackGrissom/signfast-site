Sign Any PDF Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-14. Sign Any PDF was previously called Sign Any PDF; the bundle identifier still reflects the old name.

Sign Any PDF is a PDF signing app that runs entirely on your device. We have intentionally designed it so that we cannot see, store, or transmit your documents, your signatures, or any information about how you use the app.

Data we collect

None. Sign Any PDF does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server we control. There are no accounts, no logins, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash-reporting third parties.

Data that stays on your device

The following are stored locally on your device and are never sent anywhere by Sign Any PDF:

  • PDF documents you open
  • Signatures you create (drawn, typed, or imported), and the details you save to reuse on later forms
  • App settings and preferences

File access

When you tap to open a PDF, iOS shows the file picker. Sign Any PDF can only read files you explicitly choose. We do not have, nor do we request, broad file-system access.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple via StoreKit and the App Store. Apple sends us only an anonymous receipt indicating that a purchase occurred, so the app can unlock exporting, saved details and the page tools. Pro is a single one-time purchase; there is no subscription and nothing renews. Your billing information, Apple ID, and payment method are visible only to Apple, not to us. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles your purchase data.

Third-party services

Sign Any PDF does not embed or call any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or user tracking. The app does not make any network requests during normal use. The only network activity comes from iOS itself (for example, StoreKit during a purchase).

Children

Sign Any PDF is a general-purpose utility for signing PDFs and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what Sign Any PDF does with data, we will update this page with a new "last updated" date. Because Sign Any PDF is offline-first, any future change that involves collecting data would also require a new app version that you would have to choose to install.
