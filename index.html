What it is

Sign Any PDF is the fastest way to fill in and sign a PDF on iOS. Open a file, sketch or type your signature, drop it on the page, export. No login walls, no accounts, no uploads. Your documents never leave your phone.

Signatures are visual — drawn, typed or imported, then placed onto the page. The app does not generate a cryptographic key pair and does not apply a digital certificate, so check what your recipient requires if a document needs one.
